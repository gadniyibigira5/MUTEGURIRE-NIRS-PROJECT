# Alignment with Rwanda's national priorities

Mutegurire is positioned under **Track 3: Open Innovation**, which permits solutions in health and other sectors when informed by NISR data or other open/public datasets and aligned with NST2 or Vision 2050 priorities.

## Proposed contribution

The tool supports evidence exploration for maternal and child health planning by bringing selected district indicators into a shared interface and exposing missingness and source uncertainty. It may help a planner decide which questions require further investigation or where to check whether service access is adequate. It does not establish that any observed factor causes mortality, stunting, or teenage pregnancy, and it cannot prove that a suggested intervention will improve outcomes.

## Target verification

The app currently includes contextual target statements about stunting and maternal mortality. These were included in the prototype but must be checked against the current official NST2 document before being quoted as authoritative. Record the exact document title, publication version, page, target year, unit, and wording in the source register. If this check cannot be completed before a demo, label the statements as pending verification or remove the numerical target from the public-facing copy.

## Responsible use

- Use population and health indicators to improve service access, not to stigmatize districts or families.
- Treat family-planning measures as indicators of voluntary access and unmet need, not coercive fertility targets.
- Present ranking as a screening signal, not an automatic funding decision.
- Pair district-level evidence with local context, community consultation, service availability, and programme evaluation.
- Make data gaps visible and do not imply that missing data means no need.
